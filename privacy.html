Privacy Policy

PCC SHE Workspace · Last updated 4 September 2026

What this application is

PCC SHE Workspace is a set of internal web applications built on Google Apps Script for a single construction project's safety, health and environment (SHE) team. It is not offered to the public. Only Google accounts registered by the workspace administrator can use it.

What data the application accesses

When you sign in with your Google Account, the application requests permission to:

  • See your primary Google Account email address — used only to check whether your account is on the team's registered access list and to attribute the records you submit to you.
  • Connect to an external service — used only so the web pages can call the workspace's own backend services. No third-party services receive your data.

The application does not access your Google Drive files, Gmail, Calendar, contacts, or any other Google data.

How data is used and stored

  • Your email address and the SHE records you submit (observations, findings, corrective actions, photographs) are stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive folders owned by the workspace administrator.
  • Data is used solely for the project team's SHE management, reporting and performance tracking.
  • Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or disclosed to anyone outside the project team and the administrator, except where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your access

You can revoke the application's access to your Google Account at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your account removed from the access list, contact the administrator.
